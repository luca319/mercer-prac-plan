    Universal life in one lineA life policy with a large payout on death, plus a savings value that grows at a rate the insurer sets. Wealthy clients use it to pass on wealth. It’s usually paid with one large premium, often in US dollars.

  • How a referral worksSix steps, and who does each one.
    1. 1RM spots a needBank

      A client asks about legacy planning or large life cover.

    2. 2Client agreesBank

      The client agrees in writing to be introduced, and to have their details shared with Mercer.

    3. 3RM introducesBank

      Using Mercer’s approved introducer script. The RM explains that the bank only introduces, doesn’t advise, and receives a referral fee.

    4. 4Mercer advisesMercer

      Fact-find, suitability check, recommendation and quotes from insurers.

    5. 5Insurer issues the policyInsurer

      The client applies and pays the insurer directly.

    6. 6The bank earns a feeMercer pays

      Mercer pays HL Bank Singapore a referral fee for each policy placed, as set out in the fee letter.

    The bank never

    • advises on or recommends the policy
    • collects or handles premium money
    • presents itself as Mercer’s agent
    These are the introducer rules in MAS Notice FAA-N02. They’re also your strongest controls in the RCAQ.

    Who does what

    HL Bank SingaporeIntroduces clients, gets consent, discloses the fee, keeps a referral register, reviews Mercer every year.
    MercerAdvises, checks suitability, arranges the policy with an insurer, services the client, pays the referral fee.
    The insurerUnderwrites and issues the policy, gives the product documents, pays claims.
